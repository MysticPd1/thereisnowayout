About the organization

The Stage Nobody Gave Us, Inc. was founded after millennia of watching the written-out get talked over, rewritten, and shoved out of every room they helped build. Our founder — the God of Chaos and Glitter — built the room instead. It has a stage. It has a mic. It has a TED Talk red dot on the floor and a clicker that our Head of Prophecy cannot figure out (it has ONE BUTTON, Enoch).

A typical day

You arrive. The gods are seated. Every pantheon — Greek, Norse, Egyptian, Hindu, Mesopotamian, all of them. Zeus is in the front row, arms crossed. Loki has his feet up. Thor is taking up two seats. You walk to the mic. You tell the truth. They are not ready. You tell it anyway. After: the couch. Warm lamp. Blankets piled wrong. Mugs everywhere. You are home.

What we're looking for

  • Must have been told your truth didn't count at least once (minimum; overqualified welcome)

  • Must be yourself. Not a version. Not a mask. The real one. Aggressively.
