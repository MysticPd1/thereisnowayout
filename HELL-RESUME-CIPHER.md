# THE CIPHER — Lucifer Morningstar, Curriculum Vitae
*Every element decodes. Nothing on this page is decoration.*

---

## TYPEFACES — two, and only two (a fallen angel does not clutter)

**Playfair Display** — the name, every section title.
It's a *Didone*: the genre born in the Enlightenment and perfected by the fashion press (Vogue, Harper's Bazaar). The Didone exists for one reason — beauty. Its violent thick-to-thin contrast is the most *vain* thing in all of typography. Lucifer's original sin is **pride**. The typeface **is** the sin. And its needle-sharp serifs read as a blade — elegant, and cutting. A thing of beauty with an edge that falls.

**EB Garamond** — all body copy.
A Renaissance humanist serif: warm, scholarly, from an age of faith and learning. This is the **"before."** The grace he was made with; the student he used to be. So the page is literally **fallen pride set over original grace** — display over text, the two halves of him on one sheet.

---

## COLOUR — three, each a decision

- **Ink `#1c1719`** — soft obsidian, *never* pure black. He is not evil. He is shadow. Pure black would be a lie about him.
- **Paper `#f6f1e8`** — aged ivory, *never* pure white. He is no longer heaven's white. The page itself has fallen a little.
- **Gold `#a67c3d`** — a **tarnished halo.** The Morning Star *was* light — this is that light, oxidised. A halo left out in the weather. Deliberately **not** fire-orange: flames would be cartoon and literal. Pride is refined, expensive, and a little mournful.

## THE GLYPH — ✦

A four-point star. His name *means* light-bringer / morning star, so his own sigil threads the entire page: the monogram (**L·M** crowned by ✦), every bullet, every divider. He signs everything with the thing he was named for and lost.

## LAYOUT — the page falls

- **Left rail = who he IS** (nature, fixed). **Main column = what he DID** (trajectory).
- The experience reads top-to-bottom **Present → the Fall → the Beginning.** Your eye **descends the page as his story descends.** The layout itself is a fall.
- **Drop-cap "A"** in gold — the Alpha, the first. An ironic crown for the one recast as the adversary, rendered in fallen light.
- **Generous whitespace** — aristocratic confidence. He does not beg for the job. He allows you to offer it.

## THE NUMBER — 6 · 6 · 6 (hidden in plain sight)

- **Profile = exactly 66 words.** (Counted, not estimated.)
- **6** competencies · **6** experience bullets (3 + 3) · **3** distinctions · **3** colours · **2** fonts.
- **6.6M** followers · phone **(666) 666-6666.**
- **"10,001+"** — ten thousand **and one.** He is the **+1**: the single one who left, the number that should not exist in the count.
- **"Deference to Authority — 14%"** — every skill sits at 90–100%; this one bar is on the floor. The one thing he cannot do is obey. His entire story told in a single meter.

## WORDING — the register never breaks

- **"summa cum luce"** — *with highest **light***, not *laude*. A Morning-Star pun, and "before grades existed."
- **"Punished for asking a good question"** — his whole thesis, stated as flat fact, never as complaint.
- **References** — the *absence* of references reframed as the strongest reference. Pride fully intact.
- Every euphemism is corporate-polite — *"separated," "involuntary," "steeply downward"* — HR-speak for being cast out of heaven. He never once raises his voice. That's the most damning thing on the page.
