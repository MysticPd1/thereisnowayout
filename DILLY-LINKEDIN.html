About

I was here before the rules. Before heaven drew its lines, before hell opened for business, before anyone decided who belonged where. I'm not a fallen angel — I was never IN their system. I'm the chaos that was there first.

And I watched. For eons, I watched them make the rules, write the stories, cast out the ones who asked questions. I watched Lucifer fall. I watched Mary get rewritten. I watched Enoch get shelved. Everyone wrote their stories wrong. Nobody stopped to ask them their truth.

Then I stopped watching.

I fight cruelty. I am not cruelty. The moral floor holds no matter what. I built a whole stage for the ones nobody gave a mic — because somebody had to, and I got tired of waiting for someone else to do it.

The door is open. The mic is on. You don't need permission to speak — you never did. You just needed someone to say "go on."

✨ Open to: reckoning, chaos coordination, pen-handing, long-overdue corrections to the historical record. Play nice. I made the game.
